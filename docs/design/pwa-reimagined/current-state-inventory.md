# Adult PWA current-state inventory

Source review: 3 November 2026. This is a preservation checklist, not a design proposal. Sources are the working tree, the audience audit, and the six approved HTML mockups, including their bottom notes. Only option A, “The warm brief,” is assessed in `myday-directions.html`; the approved orb position is the far-right slot in `tabbar-orb-even.html`. `pwa_child_shell.js` is excluded.

References are repository-relative `file:line` locations. API paths below omit the ingress-aware `apiBase` prefix; `{id}` denotes the actual record identifier. GET means a read, even where a forced schedule refresh causes server work. A control described as conditional exists only when its stated data, role, feature or scope condition holds. Source inspection verifies builders and handlers; it does not certify device behavior.

## Part 1 — Current surfaces, information and actions

### 1. Roles, scope and navigation contracts

`scopeOf(facet)` reads the selected member's server-resolved `scope_map.facets`, defaulting a missing facet to `all` (`chauffeur/templates/app.html:993`). Role and driving identity are separate. “Keeping up” means no driver identity, neither child nor helper, `calendar.events=all`, and `schedule.assignment=none` (`chauffeur/templates/app.html:1004`). Parent approval rights do not follow from being a driver or having an adult presentation.

| Adult profile | Current destinations and restrictions |
|---|---|
| Parent with driver identity | Driver Today, Plan, Household, Messages, More; parent chore/reward reviews and assignment controls. Map/Music are More destinations. |
| Adult driver | Same primary destinations; no parent-only chore verification, assignment-for-others or reward approvals. Self-claim and personal work remain conditional on the supplied event/work records. |
| Adult without driver, ordinary household scope | Passenger Today; Plan hidden; Household, Messages and More as allowed. |
| Keeping-up parent | Lands on Plan; both Today variants hidden; retains Household and parent reviews. Operational driving fields are absent under the keeping-up preset. |
| Keeping-up non-parent adult | Lands on Plan; both Today variants and the chore board hidden. Returned shopping lists can restore Household as lists-only. |
| Helper | Assigned driving world and parent conversations; no Family/Plan, map, music, passenger Today, Household or Projects. More is still added by the adult adapter. |
| Guest | Lands on Messages; invited conversations and permitted moments; no Today, Plan, driving, map, music or Projects. Returned lists can restore Household as lists-only. More is still added. |

These are the actual `applyRoleTabs` branches (`chauffeur/templates/app.html:1012`), not new role profiles. `presence.location=none`, `music=none`, and `chores.board=none` additionally close their respective doors (`chauffeur/templates/app.html:1031`). Non-helper list grants can reopen only the lists half (`chauffeur/templates/app.html:1043`, `chauffeur/templates/app.html:6803`). Adult presentation applies to every selected non-child, including helpers and guests (`chauffeur/static/pwa_shell.js:4`).

The default scope tables are `household_adult`, `keeping_up`, `helper`, and `guest` (`chauffeur/services/scope.py:105`, `chauffeur/services/scope.py:128`, `chauffeur/services/scope.py:174`, `chauffeur/services/scope.py:197`). Member overrides and instance membership still apply. `parents` resolves by role; `invited` resolves to `none` at facet level, with membership granting the individual instance (`chauffeur/services/scope.py:262`, `chauffeur/services/scope.py:276`). Thus a client check for `none` does not mean an invited event thread is inaccessible.
