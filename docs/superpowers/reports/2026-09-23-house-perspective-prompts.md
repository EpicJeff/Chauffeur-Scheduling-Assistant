# Living-room object perspectives

Built-in ImageGen, 2026-09-23. Each day view uses `living-day.png` as its continuity reference; each night edit uses its matching day close-up as the edit target and `living-night.png` as the lighting reference. Assets are saved in `chauffeur/static/house_hybrid/` as `{music,pets,tasks,programs}-{day,night}.png`.

Walk toward that left bookshelf and lower the camera to the radio's height. Three-quarter near-front view with a little top and right depth visible; radio large, centered around x34%, y49%, occupying about 35% image width. Keep the books and pottery next to it and the oak shelf edge. A sliver of limestone fireplace belongs at the far RIGHT, not the left. Background shelves above/below create spatial depth. Do not move the radio to a desk or change its design.
