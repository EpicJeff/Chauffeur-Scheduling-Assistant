How far ahead

Saved ✓
Number of days into the future to display on the Kiosk Mode Dashboard (1-14)
How far ahead the solver actually assigns drivers. Displaying more days than this shows them unassigned.

Solver Behavior

Saved ✓
Balance Driving Load

When on, the solver spreads events across all drivers using the chosen measure of load. When off, it fills the highest-priority drivers first. Attendee assignments, manual overrides, and shared-passenger chains still take precedence. Driving time is estimated as the round trip from each driver's home to the event.

Suggested Driver Routes

When on, events no real driver can take get a suggested "extra driver" route (ghost columns on the dashboard). When off, those events just stay in the unassigned triage bucket.

Routing & Traffic

Mapbox is switched off in Config → General, so drives are priced by the free routing servers and these two do nothing until it is back on.

Day-of traffic. Re-price each drive with real traffic on its day: a predictive pass in the morning and a live check an hour before leaving. Leave-by times and Time-to-Leave pushes only ever move earlier. Two small Directions requests per drive per day; planning stays on typical times.
Hour of the morning pass (0–23). The day's drives get their predictive estimates at this hour.
Avoid toll roads. Price every drive without tolls — otherwise Mapbox quotes the toll route's faster time whether or not you would take it. Flipping this clears every cached drive time and re-prices the schedule under the new policy.

The evening before

Saved ✓
Tomorrow Digest

Each evening, subscribed drivers get one push listing tomorrow's drives (with any prep-kit items to bring) topped by a forecast line from the Home Assistant weather integration chosen in Config → General; the line is simply omitted when Home Assistant is unreachable.
