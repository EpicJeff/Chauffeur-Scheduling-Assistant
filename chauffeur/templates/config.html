Family Chat Assistant

Proactive Chat Suggestions

When on, Argyle watches the family chat for action-shaped messages ("I can't drive Thursday", "we're out of milk") and offers a one-tap suggestion card. It never acts on its own — a parent always approves. Requires an AI key above. You can always summon Argyle directly with @argyle.

Solver Behavior

Balance Driving Load

When on, the solver spreads events across all drivers using the chosen measure of load. When off, it fills the highest-priority drivers first. Attendee assignments, manual overrides, and shared-passenger chains still take precedence. Driving time is estimated as the round trip from each driver's home to the event.

Suggested Driver Routes

When on, events no real driver can take get a suggested "extra driver" route (ghost columns on the dashboard). When off, those events just stay in the unassigned triage bucket.

Digests & Weather

Tomorrow Digest

Each evening, subscribed drivers get one push listing tomorrow's drives (with any prep-kit items to bring) topped by a forecast line from the chosen Home Assistant weather integration. Auto-detect uses the first weather entity; the line is simply omitted when Home Assistant is unreachable.

Weekly Family Digest

Once a week Argyle posts a "Family Week in Review" into the family chat: drives per driver, kids' activity counts, chores verified and points earned, rewards granted, and routine completion with streaks. Everyone gets it through the normal message notifications.

Proactive Heads-ups

Argyle periodically sweeps for stuck things and DMs parents one consolidated heads-up: events with no driver in the next 3 days, intake proposals waiting 3+ days, chores awaiting your OK for 2+ days, chores unclaimed for a week, stale reward requests, past-due errands, and a weekly prep-kit idea check. Each item is mentioned exactly once, never between 9pm and 8am.

Growing up

What the app shows a child changes as they get older — how much of the week they can see, whether points and streaks lead the screen, whether they can ask you for things, whether they can be given real jobs. Birthdays live on each person's identity card above; a birthday only suggests a stage, and you can pin any child wherever suits them. Nothing is ever deleted when a child moves up — points and streaks are kept, they just stop being the point.

Outside hands
