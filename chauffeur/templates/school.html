School

What a school day is, each child's hours and bus, the evening look at tomorrow, and the stages a child grows through. Parents only.

🏫 Each child

School hours, aftercare, the bus and school task feeds are on each child's card in Config → People for now.

🏫 School Calendar Saved ✓
to

Tells Chauffeur what a school day is. School-day features (the after-school pickup push, bus leave-by lines) run on weekdays inside the school year, skipping any day the chosen calendar has an all-day event matching a no-school keyword. The school year is detected automatically from the calendar's "First/Last Day of School" events — the override dates are only for calendars without those markers (setting either date takes full manual control). A district Google calendar works directly — paste its calendar ID (public calendars just work; a private one must be shared with the same service account as your family calendars). An ICS-fed calendar works too. Nothing set = plain weekdays, as before.

🔤 The words on your district's calendar

Comma-separated. An event matches when its title contains any of the words, ignoring capitals. Closures, half days and late starts read all-day events only; first and last day of school read any event. Empty a box to go back to the usual words.

🌙 Kid Digest
to
Saved ✓

Each child gets a "🌙 Tomorrow" Argyle DM — their rides with who's driving, what to bring, weather, and their streak — and the same cards show on the routines kiosk board, so kids without phones see it on the wall panel. Kids with nothing tomorrow get nothing. Nothing kid-facing ever sends during kid quiet hours; a send time inside the quiet window means the digest is skipped that day.

🌱 Growing up

What the app shows a child changes as they get older — how much of the week they can see, whether points and streaks lead the screen, whether they can ask you for things, whether they can be given real jobs. Birthdays live on each person's identity card in Config → People; a birthday only suggests a stage, and you can pin any child wherever suits them. Nothing is ever deleted when a child moves up — points and streaks are kept, they just stop being the point.
