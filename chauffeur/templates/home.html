🖥️ Wall panel setup

Open any page with ?panel=true to turn this install into a wall panel: the top bar becomes a shelf of touch-sized buttons along the bottom and everything sized for a mouse gets bigger. What follows is what a panel shows when its address says nothing more than that — a display bolted to a wall should not need a bookmark only its author can maintain. A ?widgets= or ?tabs= on the URL still wins, so a Home Assistant card can differ.

Home board tiles

In the order they appear. A tile with nothing to say hides itself regardless — an empty box is worse than a shorter board.

None picked — the panel will show every tile but Intake.
Shelf buttons

Left to right along the bottom, in this order. Six or seven fit at a size a thumb can hit on a 10" display and the rest move into a More button — so the order is also the choice of which destinations stay one tap away.

None picked — the panel will show every destination but Intake.
Background

The picture behind every page that has not been given its own above. Paste a web address, or just type what you want — mountains at dusk, our town — and one gets found for you. Leave it empty for the plain gradient.

Theme

Auto follows the device, which under Home Assistant means following the dashboard the panel is embedded in.

Return home when idle

How long the panel sits untouched somewhere else before it goes back to this board. Zero leaves it wherever it was left.
