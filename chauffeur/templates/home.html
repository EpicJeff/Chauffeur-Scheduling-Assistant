🖥️ Wall panel setup

Open any page with ?panel=true to turn this install into a wall panel: the top bar becomes a shelf of touch-sized buttons along the bottom and everything sized for a mouse gets bigger. What follows is what a panel shows when its address says nothing more than that — a display bolted to a wall should not need a bookmark only its author can maintain. A ?widgets= or ?tabs= on the URL still wins, so a Home Assistant card can differ.

What a tap may do

Tiles and cards can toggle lights, switches, fans and helpers when the tile itself allows tapping. Locks, covers, garage doors and valves are refused unless you say otherwise here — a wall panel is reachable by everybody in the house. Alarms are never included and this does not change that.

Background

The picture behind every page that has not been given its own above. Paste a web address, or just type what you want — mountains at dusk, our town — and one gets found for you. Leave it empty for the plain gradient.

Leave this empty if you open the panel through Home Assistant — the frame is then on HA's own origin, which is the only arrangement where it reliably loads. On a standalone panel HA usually refuses to be framed by another origin whatever you put here.

Theme

Auto follows the device — which inside a Home Assistant dashboard means following HA, but outside one (the app over your tunnel, a browser pointed straight at the panel) it can only see the tablet, and a tablet that was never told about dark mode says light at every hour. Sun asks Home Assistant where the sun actually is, so it reads the same on the wall and away from the house.

Shift either switch to taste. These are two numbers rather than one because they are not the same amount of sky: going dark 30 minutes after sunset matches going light 30 minutes before sunrise, so that pair is +30 and −30.
