🖥️ Wall panel setup

Open any page with ?panel=true to turn this install into a wall panel: the top bar becomes a shelf of touch-sized buttons along the bottom and everything sized for a mouse gets bigger. What follows is what a panel shows when its address says nothing more than that — a display bolted to a wall should not need a bookmark only its author can maintain. A ?widgets= or ?tabs= on the URL still wins, so a Home Assistant card can differ.
