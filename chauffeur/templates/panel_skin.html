{# The wall panel's design system, for EVERY page. The home board shipped its own look and nothing else got it, so tapping DRIVES on the shelf dropped you out of a photographic display and into a flat dark web page. A skin that stops at one surface is worse than no skin — it makes the other ten look broken rather than plain. Rewriting eleven templates was not the answer. This file does what ha_theme.html has always done for Home Assistant themes — map the Tailwind greys the pages are already written in onto a small set of tokens — except the tokens here are ours and they carry light/dark with them. A page written a year ago in `bg-gray-800` becomes glass on a photograph without being touched, and keeps working exactly as before outside panel mode. Everything is scoped to `html[data-panel]` on purpose. In an ordinary browser the admin pages stay exactly as they are: the panel is where the look matters, and silently restyling every page of a working app for people sitting at a laptop is a much bigger promise than the one being made here. #}
