{# Pairing a wall panel (auth arc S6). Opened with ?enrol=1 on the panel itself. A parent makes a code in Config → People → Trusted devices, reads it across the room, and the screen types it once and never again. Why a code rather than a login: a panel has no keyboard worth using, nobody signs in on it, and asking somebody to type an email and a password on a touch screen in a hallway is the kind of thing that gets done once and then worked around forever. It is also the honest shape — the panel is not a person signing in, it is a place being vouched for. #}
