{# The Boards tab. A list of every board this household can put on the wall — theirs, the ones the app ships, and Home — in the order they sit along the panel shelf, with a switch for whether each one gets a button and a house marker for which one is home. Clicking a board opens it, in edit mode, which is where every board setting now lives (B1/B2). DRAGGED, not nudged with arrows. An ordering control that cannot show you the order is the long way round, and this list is the order. Pointer events rather than HTML5 drag-and-drop, for the same reason the board's own tiles use them: dragstart does not fire for touch, and this is a thing people will do on a tablet. Its own component with its own Alpine scope, POSTing only its own keys to /api/settings, so config.html grows by an include rather than by a thousand lines of somebody else's feature. #}
