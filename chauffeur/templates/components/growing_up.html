🌱 Growing up

The stages a child grows through and what each one changes. Parents only.

What the app shows a child changes as they get older — how much of the week they can see, whether points and streaks lead the screen, whether they can ask you for things, whether they can be given real jobs. Birthdays live on each person's identity card in Config → People; a birthday only suggests a stage, and you can pin any child wherever suits them. Nothing is ever deleted when a child moves up — points and streaks are kept, they just stop being the point.
