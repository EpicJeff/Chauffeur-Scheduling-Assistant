{# ── The family calendar grid, mountable more than once. FullCalendar's month, week and day views, pulled out of the calendar page so the board can mount one as a card — the same move `components/schedule_timeline.html` made for the Drives timeline, and for the same reason: the alternative is a second, smaller calendar that drifts away from the real one over a year. What did NOT come with it, deliberately: the agenda panel, the people legend, and the two modals. Those are the calendar PAGE's chrome. The component takes them as optional callbacks (`onAgenda`, `onEventClick`) and works without any of them, which is what lets a card mount one on a wall panel where there is nothing to click through to. The library is 282KB and is injected ON FIRST USE, never by simply including this file. A board showing an agenda-only calendar card downloads none of it. #}
