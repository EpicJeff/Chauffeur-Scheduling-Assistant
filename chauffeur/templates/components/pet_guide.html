How to play
Critters, chores and battles

You get a critter. It's free.

Everybody gets one. Pick how it looks — body, horns, eyes, colours. Changing how it looks never costs anything, ever. Make it weird if you want.

Chores and routines make it stronger.

Every chore you finish and every routine you tick off gives your critter XP. XP makes it level up, and each level gives you training points to make it tougher, faster or hit harder.

🧹 chore → ⭐ points and ✨ XP. You never have to choose between them.

Every element beats one and loses to one.

There is no best element. Pick the one you like. The arrows show who beats who — and it goes all the way round.

Battle for more XP.

Tap ⚔️ Battle. Fight a practice opponent, or ask somebody in your family. They have to say yes first — nobody can drag you into a fight.

Family battles are level-matched. Both critters drop to the same level, so it does not matter who has done more chores. It is down to your elements, your moves and a bit of luck.

You get XP even when you lose. Trying always pays.

Spend XP on new moves.

Your critter already knows four moves from its own element, free. Buy a move from a different element to cover the thing that beats you — that is the clever bit.

Save up enough and you can get a second critter. It starts at your level, so it is never a weakling.

Things that will never happen

  • • Your critter can never get sick, hungry or sad. You cannot forget to feed it.
  • • Nothing you have earned is ever taken away.
  • • Your XP is not your chore points. Spending XP never costs you points, and battling never earns them.
  • • Nobody keeps score of who wins. There is no leaderboard.
