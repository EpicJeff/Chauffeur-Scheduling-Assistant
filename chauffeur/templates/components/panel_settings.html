{# The wall panel's own settings — true of every board rather than of any one of them. These were the right-hand column of the old `#panel-setup` block on the home board; they live beside the board list now, because that is where somebody thinking about the wall is. Reads and writes `panel` on the surrounding Alpine scope (boardsAdmin), and calls `savePanel()`. No state of its own. #}
Background

The photograph every board floats on unless it has one of its own.

Pictures for the app's boards

Those boards come with the app, so their tiles are not yours to move — but the photograph is. Blank uses the background above.

Theme

Auto follows the device, which inside a Home Assistant dashboard means following HA — but a standalone tablet answers “light” to that question whatever time it is, which is what Sun exists for.

Return home when idle

A panel somebody walked away from goes back to the home board. 0 turns it off. Home is whichever board wears the ⌂ above.

Screensaver
What a tap may do

Off, those tiles are a display. On, a tap on the wall does the thing — which is right for a light and wrong for a garage door, so it is one decision made deliberately rather than a default.

Home Assistant dashboard address

Leave empty if you open the panel through Home Assistant — the frame is then on HA's own origin, which is the only arrangement where it reliably loads. On a standalone panel HA usually refuses to be framed by another origin whatever you put here.
