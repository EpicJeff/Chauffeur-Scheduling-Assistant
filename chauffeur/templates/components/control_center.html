What can Argyle do?

Argyle is your AI scheduling assistant! You can use natural language to control almost any aspect of Chauffeur. Try asking:

Trip Planning

  • "Plan a 5-day trip to Tokyo with lots of food."
  • "Smart schedule all my unscheduled attractions."
  • "Move my dinner reservation to 8:30 PM."

Passenger Management

  • "Change Jeff's hashtag from #jeff to #j."
  • "Link Sarah's calendar to her passenger profile."
  • "Who is currently riding with me today?"

Daily Driving & Vehicles

  • "Change the Highlander's home location to my house."
  • "Set a custom schedule for Friday from 9 AM to 5 PM."
  • "What is my driving schedule looking like for tomorrow?"

Smart Rules & Constraints

  • "Set a rule to merge duplicate events if they are close."
  • "Limit my maximum driving distance today to 100 miles."
  • "Add a mutually exclusive rule between work and personal tasks."
