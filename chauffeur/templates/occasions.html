🎉 Occasions

A holiday, a birthday, a party. Nothing lives inside an occasion — the lists, errands and trips stay where they already are, and this is the context that ties them together and generates the work.

The window is when guests are in the house and prep is happening — it makes holiday dishes available, which is not the same as putting them on a plate. Leave it blank for the day alone.
